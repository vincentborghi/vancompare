# vancompare

Interactive utility vehicle and van comparison tool focusing on dimensions, underground parking clearance (< 1.90 m), mechanical reliability, and AdBlue SCR systems.

Designed as a decision-making guide to evaluate replacements for a Citroen Jumpy II (2008) against modern alternatives (Jumpy III M & XL, Renault Trafic III L1 & L2, Ford Transit Custom, VW Transporter T6).

## Features

- **Visual Dimension Comparison**: Scaled graphical bars comparing overall body length and usable floor length.
- **Detailed Comparison Table**: Key metrics including overall height, loading sill, turning circle radius, internal height, and Crit'Air vignette rating.
- **Side-by-Side Duel**: Dynamic delta calculator showing exact +/- centimeter differences in floor length, vehicle height, and loading capacity.
- **Engine Reliability & AdBlue Dossier**: Breakdown of known mechanical issues (Stellantis sealed AdBlue tanks, Ford wet belts, VW BiTDI EGR cooler failures, Renault M9R chain durability).
- **Interactive Simulator**: Personalized recommendation engine filtering vehicles by parking constraints, required floor length, and AdBlue tolerance.
